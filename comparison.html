What's Missing on the Current Site

  • Menu items: the menu video fails to load
  • Prices for any drink or food item
  • Online ordering or DoorDash link
  • Hours on the homepage
  • Phone numbers on the homepage
  • Address on the homepage
  • Any info about the McNichols location
  • Event space / private booking info
  • Customer reviews or testimonials
  • Their story: mother-daughter founders
  • Social media links
  • A clear call to action anywhere

What's Included in the Redesign

  • Full drink menu: 8 featured drinks with prices
  • Full food menu: 6 items with prices
  • DoorDash ordering button in the header
  • Hours on every location card, plus live open/closed status
  • Both phone numbers in the location cards and footer
  • Both addresses with Google Maps directions
  • Dedicated two-location section with the new McNichols shop
  • Event space section with booking CTA
  • Customer reviews section
  • Our Story: Beverly Beal Sr. and Jr.
  • Instagram link and social section
  • Clear CTAs throughout: View Menu, Order, Book, Visit
