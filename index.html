Our Story

Detroit Brew-ti-ful started with a simple idea: a coffee house where neighbors could gather, where the drinks are made with care, and where everybody knows your name.

Beverly Beal Sr. has run businesses in Detroit for decades. Her daughter, Beverly Beal Jr., is an attorney and entrepreneur who wanted to build something alongside her mother — something that reflected their family's connection to this city.

They opened the first shop on Plymouth Road. The community showed up. Regulars became friends. Friends became family. And in March 2026, they opened a second location on McNichols Road to serve even more of Detroit's west side.

"More than just a place to grab a cup of joe — it's a haven for those who appreciate the artistry and craftsmanship behind every brew." — The Brew-ti-ful philosophy

What makes us different

We source beans from sustainable and ethical suppliers. Our baristas are trained to craft every cup to perfection. But what really sets us apart is the room itself — cozy seating, warm light, neighbors chatting, kids doing homework, creatives working on their next thing.

This is a third place. Not home, not work — somewhere in between, where you belong.

Whether you're meeting a friend for a catch-up, working on your latest project, or just need a quiet moment with a good cup of coffee, our doors are always open to you.
