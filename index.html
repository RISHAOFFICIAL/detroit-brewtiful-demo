Our Story

A Mother-Daughter Dream, Brewed in Detroit

Detroit Brew-ti-ful Coffee House was founded by Beverly Beal Sr. and Beverly Beal Jr. — a mother-daughter team with deep roots in Detroit and a shared love of coffee, community, and good food.

What started as one cozy shop on Plymouth Road has grown into two Detroit locations, a loyal community of regulars, and a space where neighbors become friends and friends become family.

"More than just a place to grab a cup of joe — it's a haven for those who appreciate the artistry and craftsmanship behind every brew."

Every drink is crafted with care. Every pastry is baked fresh. Every customer is welcomed like family. That is the Brew-ti-ful way.
